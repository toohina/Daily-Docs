Nondescript - Lacking interesting features. Eg: He wore a nondescript suit.

Despicable - Deserving hatred or contempt.

Patently - Clearly; without a doubt. Eg: the claims were platently false.

Bristling - Aggressively, brisk or tense.
Eg: He curled his hand under the table, bristling.

Brisk - Active and Energetic. Eg: Let's go for a brisk walk.

Harbor - keep (something negative). Eg:
"she started to harbour doubts about the wisdom of their journey"
"he was suspected of harbouring an escaped prisoner"
